Our story

Built for the multi-event wedding weekend.

Our specialty is the multi-event wedding weekend: South Asian, Arab and Chaldean, Hindu and Sikh, West African and interfaith celebrations, often across several days and two faiths. We also run engagement parties, nonprofit galas and milestone celebrations.

We take a limited number of weddings each season, so every event has a lead planner and one assistant per 100 guests.
