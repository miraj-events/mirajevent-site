Meet the founder

More than a decade of planning.

Mehreen Buksh has been planning events for more than ten years. She started young, organizing youth and community events (booking speakers, building timelines and managing budgets) and went on to lead community, public, private and corporate events before focusing on weddings.

Couples know her for a calm presence, a sharp eye for detail and a habit of solving problems before anyone notices them. She works with a trusted network of vendors across Michigan.

Today her specialty is the multi-event wedding weekend: South Asian, Arab and Chaldean, Hindu and Sikh, West African and interfaith celebrations, often across several days and two faiths. We also run engagement parties, nonprofit galas and milestone celebrations.

The team

A Miraj team on every event.

We take a limited number of weddings each season, so every event has a lead planner and one assistant per 100 guests, on site from setup to send-off.

Vendors, venue and family stay on one timeline, and problems are solved before they reach you.
