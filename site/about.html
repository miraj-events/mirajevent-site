About

An experienced lead planner, on every event.

Founded and led by Mehreen Buksh, Miraj Events plans, designs and runs weddings across Metro Detroit, Toledo and Chicago. More than 50 multicultural, multi-event weddings in the past two years alone.

Our specialty: South Asian, Arab and Chaldean, Hindu and Sikh, West African and interfaith weddings, often across several days and two faiths.

We take a limited number of weddings each season. Every event has a lead planner and one assistant per 100 guests.
