Floor plan
Why this layout
  1. Stage faces the entrance, so it’s the first thing guests see.
  2. Dance floor in the middle, with every table close to the action.
  3. Bar and desserts at the back keep the stage clear for photos.
  4. One clear aisle from the door to the dance floor.
