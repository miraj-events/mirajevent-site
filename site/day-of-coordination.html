Day-of questions

Can you run our whole wedding weekend?

Yes, and it's what we do most. Any package can cover every event from the welcome party to the farewell brunch. Prices are per event, and we'll build one customized package for the whole weekend after your call.

How do payments work?

Day-of Coordination is 50% at booking and the balance two weeks before your event. For the other packages it's 25% at booking, 50% at the 90-day mark and the final 25% before the wedding weekend. The retainer is what holds your date.

What if we need to postpone or cancel?

We know plans change. Postponing is free: everything you’ve paid moves to your new date, as long as it’s within 12 months. If you cancel, the retainer is non-refundable; more than 90 days out we keep what’s been paid to date, and within 90 days the full fee is due.

Do you travel?

Yes. Metro Detroit, Toledo and Chicago are home turf. Beyond 60 miles of Detroit, travel and lodging are billed at cost, with no markup.
