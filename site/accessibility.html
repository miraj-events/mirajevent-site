Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed October 2026.

Miraj Events, LLC wants mirajevent.com to work well for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the benchmark most widely used for website accessibility in the United States.

What we have done

  • Clear page structure with headings, landmarks and a skip-to-content link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Form fields have visible labels and clear error messages.
  • No carousels or slideshows advance on their own, and animations switch off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and when zoomed to 200%.

We check the site with automated tools (axe and Google Lighthouse) and by hand with a keyboard, and we review it again whenever we make changes.

Known limitations

Some text in our brand gold, and some text placed over photos, may fall below the AA color-contrast ratio. We are reviewing these. If anything is hard to read, use the contact options below.

Need help?

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you would like information in another format, contact us and we will help. Call or text (586) 504-0207 or email admin@mirajevent.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Third-party content

Some links lead to sites we do not control, such as press articles, Google reviews and social media. Those sites are responsible for their own accessibility.
