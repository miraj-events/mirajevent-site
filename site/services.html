Questions couples ask

Can you run our whole wedding weekend?

Yes, and it's what we do most. Any package can cover every event from the welcome party to the farewell brunch. Prices are per event, and we'll build one customized package for the whole weekend after your call.

We've already booked our venue and caterer. Can you take it from here?

That's the most common place couples find us. Planning + Design picks up everything else: the remaining vendors, the budget, the design and the run of show, so you can stop carrying it alone.

Do you plan weddings that blend two cultures or faiths?

Often. Two ceremonies, two families and two sets of traditions on one timeline is our specialty. We'll make sure both sides feel at home and nothing important gets rushed.

Do you help with design?

From Design + Coordination up, yes. We shape the look of the room with you, from colors and centerpieces to the stage, and oversee the décor team that brings it to life. Décor itself is billed by the vendor. See how design direction works.

How do payments work?

Day-of Coordination is 50% at booking and the balance two weeks before your event. For the other packages it's 25% at booking, 50% at the 90-day mark and the final 25% before the wedding weekend. The retainer is what holds your date.

What if we need to postpone or cancel?

We know plans change. Postponing is free: everything you’ve paid moves to your new date, as long as it’s within 12 months. If you cancel, the retainer is non-refundable; more than 90 days out we keep what’s been paid to date, and within 90 days the full fee is due.

Do you travel?

Yes. Metro Detroit, Toledo and Chicago are home turf. Beyond 60 miles of Detroit, travel and lodging are billed at cost, with no markup.

Do you do events other than weddings?

Yes: nonprofit galas, milestone birthdays, engagement parties, corporate events and grand openings. We build a package for these after a short call. Tell us about yours →
