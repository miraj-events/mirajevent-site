Multicultural wedding questions

Which wedding traditions do you plan?

South Asian (Muslim, Hindu, Sikh and Jain), Arab and Chaldean, West African and Caribbean, and interfaith and fusion weddings. We’ve run 50+ multicultural or multi-event weddings in the past two years, the largest with 900+ guests.

Can one planner run every event of our wedding weekend?

Yes, and it’s what we do most. One lead planner runs every event on one timeline, from the welcome dinner to the farewell brunch, with one assistant per 100 guests at each event. Multi-event weekends are packaged together after your call.

Can you plan two ceremonies for an interfaith wedding?

Yes. Two ceremonies, two families and two sets of traditions on one timeline is our specialty, on one day or across the weekend. We flip the room and time the outfit changes in between, so nothing important gets rushed.

Can we bring our own caterer, officiant and family vendors?

Yes. Bring the people you trust, from your caterer and DJ to your imam, pandit or priest. Every package also includes our preferred vendor list, and using it is optional.

How much does a multicultural wedding planner cost?

Our packages start at $1,975 for Day-of Coordination, $4,750 for Design + Coordination, $6,500 for Planning + Design and $9,500 for Full Planning, per event. Multi-event weekends are packaged together after a call, based on the number of events, the guest count and the design.

Where do you plan weddings?

Across Metro Detroit (Oakland, Macomb, Wayne and Washtenaw counties), plus Toledo and Chicago. Beyond 60 miles of Detroit, travel and lodging are billed at cost, with no markup.
