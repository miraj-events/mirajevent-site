What moves the price

Five things change what you’ll pay.

Number of events

Each event has its own setup, timeline and team. A henna night, a ceremony and a reception is three events, not one. How we run multi-event weekends.

Guest count

Bigger rooms need more hands. We staff one assistant per 100 guests at every event.

How early we start

Day-of begins four weeks out. Planning packages start at signing, so we carry the vendors, the budget and the design for months.

Design

Design direction (concept, floor plan and décor team) is included from Design + Coordination up. Décor itself is billed by the vendor.

Travel

Metro Detroit, Toledo and Chicago are home turf. Beyond 60 miles of Detroit, travel and lodging are billed at cost, with no markup.

Wedding planner cost questions

How much is a day-of wedding coordinator in Detroit?

Most published starting prices in Metro Detroit fall between about $2,000 and $2,800 (October 2026). Miraj Events’ Day-of Coordination starts at $1,975 for a single event and begins four weeks before the wedding.

How much does a full-service wedding planner cost in Detroit?

Full planning in Metro Detroit typically starts around $7,500 to $15,000 or more, and full-service planning often lands near 10–15% of the total wedding budget. Miraj Events’ Full Planning starts at $9,500 for a single event.

Do wedding planners charge per event?

For multi-event weddings, many do. Our prices are per event, and multi-event weekends are packaged together after your call, so you get one customized package for the whole weekend.

What’s included in Day-of Coordination?

We begin four weeks out with vendor confirmations, your timeline and run of show, and a rehearsal walkthrough. On the day, a lead planner is on site from setup to send-off, with one assistant per 100 guests.

How do payments work?

Day-of Coordination is 50% at booking and the balance two weeks before your event. For the other packages it’s 25% at booking, 50% at the 90-day mark and the final 25% before the wedding weekend. The retainer is what holds your date.
